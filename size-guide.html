All measurements are body measurements in inches. Between sizes? We recommend sizing up for a more comfortable, relaxed fit. Bags and clutches are one size — see each product page for dimensions.

1

Bust

Measure around the fullest part of your bust, keeping the tape parallel to the floor.

2

Waist

Measure around the narrowest part of your natural waistline, just above your belly button.

3

Hip

Measure around the fullest part of your hips, roughly 8 inches below your waistline.
